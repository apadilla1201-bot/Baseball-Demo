Pitching development

Assessment. Data. Plan. Retest.

Every pitcher in the program is on an eight-week cycle. You'll always know where you are, what we're working on and when we measure again.

What we do

  1. 01

    In-person lessons & monthly program

    Two 60-minute sessions a week at the Doral facility. Each session has a throwing component, a strength or mobility component and a radar reading. Most athletes are in the monthly program; single lessons are for pitchers in-season who need a tune-up.

    Mon–Thu 3:30–9 pm · Sat 8 am–2 pm

  2. 02

    Remote 1-on-1 program

    For arms outside South Florida or in-season pitchers who can't make it to Doral. Weekly video review with voice-over, a throwing and lifting program adjusted every two weeks, and a retest protocol you run at a facility near you.

    Video due Sunday night · Program update every other Tuesday

  3. 03

    Velocity development

    Velo is a byproduct of force, sequencing and intent. We build force in the weight room, sequencing with constraint drills on high-speed video, and intent with weighted-ball and pulldown work scheduled around your season, never on top of it.

    Retest every 8 weeks · Radar on every bullpen

  4. 04

    Pitch design

    Spin rate, spin axis, induced vertical break, horizontal break, release height. We find the pitch your arm wants to throw and shape it until it misses bats. Most pitchers leave with a usable third pitch inside a cycle.

    Pitch metrics on retest days · Grip and cue log in your plan

  5. 05

    Arm care & return-to-throw

    Workload tracking, a daily arm-care routine that fits in twelve minutes, and a six-phase return-to-throw program for pitchers coming back from injury, written with your physical therapist and cleared by your doctor before we start.

    No mound until phase 4 · Soreness beyond 48 hrs pauses the plan

The process

Four steps. Every eight weeks.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Ninety minutes. Health and training history, mobility screen (hip IR/ER, shoulder total arc, thoracic rotation), a bullpen on radar and high-speed video, and a conversation about what you want and when.

  2. 02

    Data

    Radar max and average. Video at 240 fps from the open and closed side. Pitch metrics on every pitch type: velo, spin, axis, IVB, HB, release height and extension. Strength baselines in the weight room.

  3. 03

    Individualized plan

    A written eight-week plan within 48 hours: throwing program, lift, arm care, mechanical priorities (never more than two) and the pitch we're designing. Shared with the athlete and the parent.

  4. 04

    Retest

    Same bullpen, same equipment, same protocol. We compare, we write up what changed and what didn't, and we build the next eight weeks from that. The report goes to parents the same week.
