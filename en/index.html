Hitting these walls?

  1. 01

    Velo's been stuck for a year.

    You're throwing more and gaining nothing. More throwing isn't the fix. Usually it's strength, hip-shoulder separation or a mechanical leak you can only see at 240 frames a second.

  2. 02

    College coaches don't know you exist.

    You've been to four showcases and gotten zero emails back. Showcases aren't a recruiting plan. A profile with verified numbers, cut video and a targeted list is.

  3. 03

    There's no plan. Just lessons.

    Thirty minutes a week with a different drill each time isn't development. You need a program with a start, a retest date and someone accountable for the number.

  4. 04

    The arm keeps hurting.

    Soreness that doesn't go away in 48 hours is information. We don't throw through it. We find out why, fix the workload and build back on a schedule your doctor and I agree on.

FAQ

Questions I get every week.

Contact →
How old does my son need to be?+

Fourteen, or an eighth grader who's already throwing in the mid-60s. Younger than that and the best thing for his arm is to play three sports and throw a lot of different things, not sit in a bullpen with me.

How fast will he gain velocity?+

The program average is +4.1 mph over six months, but the spread is wide. A 15-year-old at 68 who hasn't lifted will gain faster than a 17-year-old at 86 who has. I'll give you a realistic range after the evaluation, not before.

Do you guarantee a college commitment?+

No, and be careful with anyone who does. What I guarantee is that you'll know by sophomore year what level is realistic, and that by senior fall you'll have a profile, a list and a process. Thirty-eight families have gotten a commitment out of that.

Can we do this remotely?+

Yes. Remote athletes send video weekly, get a program adjusted every two weeks, and are asked to come to Doral once a quarter to retest on our equipment if they're within driving distance. Athletes out of state retest at a facility near them with a shared protocol.

What does it cost?+

Evaluation is $175. The in-person monthly program is $620. Remote is $295 a month. Recruiting guidance is $150 a month as an add-on or $1,200 for a full grad-year plan. It's all on the Parents page, with what's included.

¿Hablan español?+

Sí. Grew up in a Cuban household in Kendall. Every lesson, report and parent call happens in whichever language the family speaks.
