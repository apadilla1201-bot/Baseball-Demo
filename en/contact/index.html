FAQ

The long list.

Training

How long is each session?+

Sixty minutes in the facility. Evaluations are ninety.

Can he come once a week instead of twice?+

Yes, in-season. Off-season, once a week isn't enough stimulus to move velocity and I'll tell you that up front.

Do you work with position players?+

Only on throwing. Two-way players are welcome in the pitching program; I don't coach hitting.

What should he bring?+

Glove, cleats or turf shoes, running shoes, water. We have baseballs, plyo balls and the equipment.

Do you train during the FHSAA season?+

Yes, with a reduced plan coordinated with his high school coach. I'd rather talk to the coach directly than have the athlete in the middle.

Remote

How does remote actually work?+

You film your bullpen from two angles on a phone, upload Sunday night, and get a video review with voice-over by Tuesday. Program updates every other week. Retest every eight weeks with a shared protocol.

Do I need a radar?+

Helpful, not required. A pocket radar is a worthwhile purchase for any remote athlete. Otherwise we retest at an event or facility near you.

Can remote athletes get recruiting guidance?+

Yes. Most of it is email, video and planning, which works the same anywhere.

Recruiting

When should we start?+

Sophomore year for the level conversation. Junior fall for the profile and emails. If you're a senior and uncommitted, call me this week.

Do you contact coaches for us?+

I'll talk to any college coach who calls me about an athlete, and I'm copied on the athlete's emails. But the first email always comes from the athlete.

What about the NCAA rules?+

They change. I'll point you to the current NCAA, NAIA and NJCAA guides for contact periods and eligibility rather than quote something that might be out of date.

Money

Is there a contract?+

No. Monthly programs cancel with two weeks' notice.

Do you offer payment plans or sibling discounts?+

Siblings in the program at the same time get 15% off the second athlete. Payment plans for the grad-year recruiting plan, yes, ask.

Does the evaluation fee get applied?+

Yes, to your first month if you join within 30 days.
